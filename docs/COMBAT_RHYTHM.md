# Combat rhythm and clarity

Build `combat.2026-10-09`, 9 October 2026. Launch **Play Overload.cmd** from either installed folder. This pass develops the original game's combat and onboarding using resource management, responsive input and readable effects as the Diablo comparison points. Blizzard's [combat readability discussion](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23746639/diablo-iv-quarterly-updatedecember-2021) and [resource/skill examples](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24130178/the-2-0-ptr-what-you-need-to-know) are design references; Diablo IV itself was not run in this workspace.

## Play

- Click **New character**, choose Warden (melee), Threadseer (spellcaster) or Revenant (ranged), and enter safe Hearth directly. Speak to Mara and follow the gold quest route.
- Left click ground to walk, a landmark to approach/interact, or an enemy to approach/attack. Hold LMB to keep attacking. Drag onto another enemy to change targets. After a kill, the held order acquires a visible, engaged enemy within 96 world pixels of the cursor with clear sight. It does not chase distant or unseen monsters. Alt holds your ground. WASD cancels the mouse order.
- Basic hits restore **8 Focus** and add one **Surge** charge. Three charges empower the next damaging paid skill by **50%**. The three pips, skill borders and cost captions show readiness. Another basic hit refreshes the six-second timer. Defensive skills with no damage, healing and evade leave charges intact. Red Covenant still builds Surge but does not restore Focus.
- Hold Q/E/R or X/Y/B to repeat equipped skills when ready. With a mouse target and LMB held, basic attacks continue while the held paid skill is on cooldown or lacks Focus. Releases retain only the existing short input buffer. Menus suppress held actions until released, and disconnects release input.
- Every third ordinary enemy defeated in an adventure area drops a red **Life globe**. While hurt, approach within 84 pixels with clear sight to attract it. It restores **12% of current maximum Life**, capped at the maximum. Globes do not refill flasks or revive the dead. They are temporary area objects and clear on travel/retry; equipment drops remain saved.
- Space evades; F heals. I compares/equips gear, K trains skills, J explains quests, Tab opens the local map, M the world atlas, and P the town portal.

## Clarity and environments

The title puts continuing, starting a character, saved characters and a concise three-step guide ahead of advanced systems. Town services retain access to equipment/forge/salvage, skills, memories, oaths and Fractures. New class cards describe range and play style. Controls name the actual equipped abilities. Skill training focuses the first available training button, and early characters no longer see post-level-60 Resonance allocation controls.

Frontier trails and Glass/Hollow outdoor roads use dirt, stone marks and irregular verges rather than repeated raised gantries. Navigation and collision continue to use the same physical geography. Ground drawing commands are retained between 64-pixel camera cells with overscan; world transactions refresh the layer immediately. Actors, wind, particles and combat effects continue to animate every frame. The cache contains one visible ground layer, rather than accumulating the visited world.

## Authority and validation

Focus and Surge reward one validated primary hit per committed basic action, including an Overloaded basic. Sweeps, piercing bolts and split projectiles share that receipt. Misses, invented roots, secondary explosions and repeat effects cannot earn charges. Receipt history is bounded to 128 actions. Successful paid-action commitment captures the damage multiplier once; rejected attempts and previews do not consume it. Retries/death clear transient combat state. Equipment changes retain current state. The save schema remains 8 because this transient state is not saved.

`build/test.ps1` verifies the domain/content model. `build/export.ps1 -SmokeTest` verifies the standalone regression suites. `build/adventure.ps1 -Rendered -Exported` exercises all three starters and headless enforcers, skills, loot, portal and reloads. `build/campaign.ps1 -Rendered -Exported` exercises the full normal-stat Warden campaign, including real mouse selection, a held order killing successive enemies, charging/consuming Surge, eight bosses, the ending and a frontier contract. `build/review-rpg.ps1 -Rendered -Exported` captures menus at three resolutions and both text sizes. `build/world.ps1 -Performance -Exported` measures the separate sixteen-actor rendering fixture.
