# A03 candidate checks

Keep the whole exported Windows folder together and launch Overload.exe. S06's independent control/comprehension session remains open; its original feedback sheet is included. Record input device, Frame, build, activity, tier and assistance setting. Do not include personal names or machine identifiers.

At Hearth choose **Create separate Standard character**, then Warden, Threadseer or Revenant. Standard starts at level 1 with its own save. **Compare accelerated training builds** creates one of nine separate level-40 fixtures with identical equipment and point budgets; their sandbox resources cannot earn a Standard ritual. Earlier characters keep their court checkpoint. New characters play the four-region campaign and face First Pattern at the final checkpoint.

1. Play each Frame's three training builds. Use basic + Q/E/R, swap skills at Hearth, rank skills, compare gear, respec and reload. Describe the action sequence and encounter where each build helps. Record weak or redundant skills; model wins do not answer whether these builds are enjoyable.
2. With Threadseer, place Ember Well and Storm Loom along aim. Move away before they resolve; their centers must stay fixed. Confirm walls block damage and secondary pulses do not generate memories. Use Tether on an ordinary foe and boss; observe slow versus additional stagger.
3. With Revenant, command Echo Order. Confirm one echo, three bounded bolts, no autonomous army, and no memory from echo damage. Bone Volley may hit multiple targets, but each victim receives that base action only once. Veil is a barrier, not a dodge or heal.
4. Visit all four regions and both bosses in each. Check silhouette recognition, locked tells, hazard borders, stagger windows, reachable enemies and ordinary traversal. Test Elsewhere returns around every obstacle arrangement. Report any blocked, ambiguous or unavoidable encounter.
5. Run the Red Covenant mastery challenge with each Frame. Stage 1 requires 20% Life reserved. Stage 2: reserve first, take a hit, then Flask while the reservation is live. Stage 3 defeats the boss. Failure costs time only; mastery grants no power or currency.
6. On a qualified Standard character, earn the first oath and Covenant mastery, then pay 30 Seals from each region. Reload after payment. Equip Covenant, swap to Elsewhere, and disable it. Confirm only one can be active.
7. Compare equivalent builds with and without Covenant. Check the 40% cap, independent four-second expiries, healing at reduced capacity, and no healing from expiry. Focus regeneration must be marked inactive. Explain why you would choose either contract; report trivialized boss mechanics.
8. Finish the new campaign, inspect its ending and Atlas unlock, quit/reload at checkpoints, and continue a regional expedition/chain. Old saved expeditions should retain their layout and banked rewards.

Unfinished is deliberately cut from this candidate. Art uses original finite SVG silhouettes and procedural regional scenery; final animation quality and performance remain review gates.

Feedback: build/archive hash; tested profile/device; reproduction steps; expected/actual behavior; control/comprehension difficulties; preferred build and oath with reasons; deaths that seemed unavoidable; reason for stopping.
