# Endless foundation through E05

6 October 2026. The owner explicitly requested implementation through E05 while the independent S06 playtest is pending. This extends implementation authorization; it does not count as human acceptance of S06 or M5.

The progression model calls the same domain formulas as combat and saving. Exact BigInteger quantities are rounded once at the final subunit boundary. Shared grade scales Life and damage, while grade-dependent defense and tier-dependent K use an exact common-denominator cancellation. Action speed, Focus, warnings, projectile counts and stagger remain fixed. Bulk forge quotes sum the arithmetic series and debit both wallets with the grade in one transaction.

The board unlocks after the eight-room court. The E03 playable fixture assembles six ordinary authored rooms and the existing boss, with immutable budgets independent of player level. Each expedition persists a unique ID, monotonic sequence, content version, seed, route, room order and contiguous reward claims. Only completion advances the frontier; replayed lower tiers and stale claims cannot advance it again. Group rewards survive abandonment, while completion wallets and targeted gear require the boss. Full bags forfeit targeted Fracture gear, explicitly disclosed by the board. Court full-bag conversion remains as previously authored.

Chapter offers are part of the completion transaction, so reload cannot reroll them. Four finite routes target equipment families and change seeded room assembly. Previously chosen routes remain selectable. These routes do not implement world-rule pairs, regional proofs or activity families. Null chain ID and zero leg reserve saved metadata; three-leg chains remain P07.

Schema 3 migrates earlier characters without changing XP, gear or wallets. Active run content versions are checked before loading to avoid silently reinterpreting a saved expedition. A 4 MiB file-size guard bounds operational parsing without imposing a designed level or tier maximum. Large counters have abbreviated display and a copyable exact ledger.

Practice CLI profiles are isolated under the test-save root. They provide reference-level entry-grade tiers 9–11 for inspection without editing normal saves or granting production oath qualification. Runtime sweeps and fixture-driven engine checks are automated evidence, not claims of player victory, duration or enjoyment. EP10 procedural fairness and EP11 normalized production trial coverage depend on later P tasks. See [E05 validation](../E05_VALIDATION.md) for evidence and limitations.
