# Saved external slice

6 October 2026. User authorization extends implementation through S06. Independent playtest arranged by the owner; evidence pending.

The slice expands the existing authoritative resolver rather than introducing another combat path. Six Assault skills share explicit Pursuit/Afterstrike adapters. Convergence authors a 15-tick tell, a terrain-colliding pull of ordinary enemies, and one 135% cone. Since Godot's broadphase retains pre-pull transforms until the next physics sync, the immediate cone intersects current actor footprints against the same authored polygon. An engine regression covers the pulled target being hit and boss displacement immunity.

Shelter wraps both standard Traverse and Elsewhere's selected phase. Its departure field lasts 120 ticks and intercepts two ordinary hostile projectiles; boss projectiles explicitly opt out. Reprieve spends one flask and Echo for 25% healing and a 15% barrier lasting 180 ticks. Full-Life use is legal only when Reprieve is actually executable.

Character aggregates use canonical decimal strings, checksummed envelopes, a same-directory flushed temporary file, atomic Windows replacement and three backups. A lock file serializes writers; revision checks and a bounded receipt ledger reject stale or duplicated commands. Recovery selects the newest valid complete snapshot, promotes it before subsequent writes, and preserves corrupt files. Future schemas block loading. Recovery UI can create a separate profile. Schema 1 migration preserves exact XP and disables unknown bindings.

The eight authored rooms are a saved checkpoint journey. Each clear transaction includes XP, wallets, deterministic item identity, unique skill milestone and next-room checkpoint. The run/room receipt and claimed-room set prevent retry/reload duplication. Full bags explicitly convert the new drop to 25 gold and 5 Alloy. Temporary Life, flasks, memories, Strain, effects and anchors are never persisted; checkpoint entry restores supplies. Existing four-encounter practice remains reward-free.

The accelerated slice starts at level 17 with 500 gold, 100 Alloy, six starter items and 120 synthetic Seals per region. Oath ownership and its four-wallet debit are one sandbox transaction. This is deliberately labeled in the UI; production level/proof/trial qualification is not implemented here. Repeatable XP is uncapped; foundation budgets remain finite, four first-clear milestones are unique, and post-60 Resonance uses exact integers. Save tests cross level 1,000 and much larger synthetic values.

Representative visuals are reproducible original SVGs; audio is deterministic oscillator/noise synthesis. Their generator and provenance manifest are in the repository. Combat geometry remains code-authored for accurate warnings. Physical controller feel, audio quality, duration, difficulty and comprehension require human evidence; automated fixture kills do not prove anyone can defeat the boss.
